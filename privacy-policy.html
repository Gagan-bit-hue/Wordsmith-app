Privacy Policy — Wordsmith

Last updated: September 2026

Wordsmith is an English learning app. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Wordsmith does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

What's stored on your device

Your progress — words learned, quiz scores, streak, and points — is saved only in your own browser or device storage, using standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Wordsmith does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device permission.

Third parties

Wordsmith does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email listed on the app's store page.
